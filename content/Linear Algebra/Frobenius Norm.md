The Frobenius norm of a matrix is equivalent to the Euclidean $L_{2}$ norm applied to a flattened matrix.

For a matrix $A$, we have:
$$
|| A ||_{F} = \sqrt{ \sum_{i,j} \left| a_{ij} \right|^{2}  }
$$
