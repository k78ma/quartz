> [!definition] Injectivity
> A function $T\, : \,V\to W$ is called *injective* if $Tu=Tv$ implies $u=v$.

This could be rephrased to say that $T$ is injective if $u \neq v$ implies that $Tu \neq Tv$. Thus, $T$ is injective if and only if it maps distinctive inputs to distinctive outputs. 

We could also say that $T$ is **one-to-one.**
