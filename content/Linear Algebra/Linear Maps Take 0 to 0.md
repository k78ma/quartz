> [!theorem] Linear maps take $0$ to $0$
> Suppose $T$ is a linear map from $V$ to $W$. Then $T(0)=0$.

*Proof.*

By additivity, we have
$$
T(0)=T(0+0)=T(0)+T(0)
$$
Add the additive inverse of $T(0)$ to each side of the equation above to conclude $T(0)=0$.
