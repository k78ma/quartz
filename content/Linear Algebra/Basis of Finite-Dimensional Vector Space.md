The fact that [[Every Spanning List Contains a Basis|every spanning list contains a basis]] leads us to this important result:

> [!theorem] Basis of Finite-Dimensional Vector Space
> Every finite-dimensional vector space has a basis.

*Proof.* By definition, a finite-dimensional vector space has a spanning list. We just saw that each spanning list can be reduced to a basis.
