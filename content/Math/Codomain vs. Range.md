Recall that in the notation $f:X\to Y$, the codomain of the function is $Y$.

On the other hand, the range of $f$ is the subset of the codomain that contains all values $y\in Y$ that are mapped to by at least one element $x \in X$.

![[Codomain vs. Range-1767542531316.webp]]

If we remove the element 3 from the domain, then 3 will be removed from the range as well. But the codomain stays the same.
