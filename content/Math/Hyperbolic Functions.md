## Hyperbolic Sine
$$
\sinh x=\frac{1}{2}(e^{x}-e^{-x})
$$
- Domain: $x \in (-\infty, \infty)$
- Range: $y \in (-\infty, \infty)$
- Odd function

## Hyperbolic Cos
$$
\cosh x= \frac{1}{2}(e^{x}+e^{-x})
$$
- Domain: $x \in(-\infty, \infty)$
- Range: $y \geq 1$
- Even function

![[Hyperbolic Functions-1767716294159.webp]]


## Hyperbolic Tan
$$
\begin{align}
\tanh x  & = \frac{e^{x}-e^{-x}}{e^{x}+e^{-x}} \\[2ex] 
     & = \frac{e^{2x}-1}{e^{2x}+1} \\[2ex] 
     & = \frac{1-e^{-2x}}{1+e^{-2x}}
\end{align}
$$
