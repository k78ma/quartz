Since the total [[Three-Phase Instantaneous Power|three-phase instantaneous power]] is constant, it is just the average power:
$$
P_{\text{3-phase}}=3V_{\text{phase}}I_{\text{phase}}\cos \theta=3\times P_{1-\text{phase}}
$$
