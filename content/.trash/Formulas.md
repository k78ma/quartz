- Rotational Newton's 2nd Law:
$$
\begin{align}
M  & = T = I\dot{\omega} \\[2ex] 
\text{KE}  & = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^{2}
\end{align}
$$
- Rotational inertia of a point mass at a distance $R$ from axis of rotation:
$$
I = mR^{2}
$$
- Inertia of a solid cylinder/disk:
$$
I = \frac{mR^{2}}{2}
$$
- Parallel springs (same deflection):
$$
k_{e}=k_{1}+k_{2}
$$
- Series springs (same force): 
$$
\frac{1}{k_{e}}=\frac{1}{k_{1}}+\frac{1}{k_{2}}
$$
- Maximum velocity spring:
$$
\dot{x}_{\text{max}}=A\omega_{n}
$$
