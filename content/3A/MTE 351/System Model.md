A *system* is a collection of interacting components of interest separated from the environment by a physical/virtual boundary.

![[System Model.png|396]]

A *dynamic system* is a system where the rate of change of its states or responses with time cannot be neglected. We deal with [[Causal and Non-Causal Systems|causal systems]] with memory.

## Modeling
Abstractions and simplifications all the way down.

![[System Model-1.png]]

Models of dynamic systems can be used for:
- Design/synthesis
- Analysis/prediction
- Control
- Diagnosis/health monitoring
- System identification
