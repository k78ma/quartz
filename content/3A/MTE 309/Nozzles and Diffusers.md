A nozzle increases the velocity of a fluid at the expense of pressure.

![[Nozzles and Diffusers.png|388]]

A diffuser increases the pressure of a fluid by slowing it down.

![[Nozzles and Diffusers-1.png|376]]

Properties:
- Adiabatic ($\dot{Q}\approx 0$). The fluid has high velocities, and thus it does not spend enough time in the device for any significant heat transfer to take place.
- No work ($\dot{W}=0$).
- Potential energy is negligible, $\Delta \text{PE}\simeq 0$.
- The change in kinetic energy must be accounted for, $\Delta \text{KE} \not\equiv 0$, as the fluid passing through the nozzle/diffuser experiences large changes in velocity.
