A nozzle increases the velocity of a fluid at the expense of pressure.

![[Nozzles and Diffusers.png|388]]

A diffuser increases the pressure of a fluid by slowing it down.

![[Nozzles and Diffusers-1.png|376]]

Typically:
- Adiabatic ($\dot{Q}\approx 0$)
- No work ($\dot{W}=0$)
- Potential energy is approximately zero, $\Delta \text{PE}\simeq 0$
- The change in kinetic energy is not identically zero, $\Delta \text{KE} \not\equiv 0$.
