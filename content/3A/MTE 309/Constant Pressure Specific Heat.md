Since the pressure is constant, not just the volume, some of the energy transfer goes into changing the volume, not just the temperature, such that:
$$
q=\Delta(u+PV)=\Delta h
$$
This is given by:
$$
\begin{align}
\int  \, du & =\int c_{v}(T) \, dT   \\[2ex]
dh & =c_{p}\;dT \\[2ex]
h_{2}-h_{1} & =\bar{c_{p}}(T_{2}-T_{1})
\end{align}
$$
where $\bar{c_{p}}$ is the average $c$ within $\Delta T$ (valid if $\Delta T$ is small).
