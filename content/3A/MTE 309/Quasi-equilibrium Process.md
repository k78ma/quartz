A system will inherently go through intermediate non-equilibrium states during a [[Thermodynamic Process|process]].

A quasi-equilibrium process is *sufficiently slow*, such that the system remains infinitesimally close to [[Equilibrium State|thermodynamic equilibrium]] throughout.

## Examples
- Nickel ball that is cooling in such a way that it has uniform temperature distribution.

![[Quasi-equilibrium Process.png|528]]

- Piston motion in a car engine is a slow process relative to air molecule motion, such that it can be considered a quasi-equilibrium process.
