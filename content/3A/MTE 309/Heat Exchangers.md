Heat exchangers can transfer transfer heat between two fluids:

![[Heat Exchangers.png]]

Typically:
- Adiabatic outside ($\dot{Q}\approx 0$)
- No work ($\dot{W}=0$)
- $\Delta \text{PE}\cong 0$ (effectively zero for practical purposes)
- $\Delta \text{KE}\approx 0$ (approximately equal to zero)
