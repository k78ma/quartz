Recall that:
$$
\dot{Q}_{\text{conv}}=\bar{h}A(T_{s}-T_{\infty})
$$
At time, we would like to increase $\dot{Q}$ without increasing $\Delta T$ or $h$. Thus, we can increase the surface area by attaching to the surface extended surfaces called **fins** made of highly conductive materials such as aluminum.
