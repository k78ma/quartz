A *continuum* is continuous, homogeneous matter with no holes.

The size of a system must be large relative to space between molecules for the continuum assumption to apply. The example below would not be considered a continuum because it doesn't contain enough molecules.

![[Continuum.png|500]]


Our density of this would be:
$$
\text{Density}= \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}=\frac{\text{\# of molecules}\times m}{V}
$$
