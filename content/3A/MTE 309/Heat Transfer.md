- Deals with specifics of one form of energy transfer – [[Thermal Energy|thermal energy]]
- Thermal energy transfer over space and time e.g. adding insulation in specific locations to decrease the heat transfer rate and save heating costs

Three modes of heat transfer:
- Conduction (molecular diffusion)
- Convection (bulk fluid motion)
- Radiation (electromagnetic waves)
