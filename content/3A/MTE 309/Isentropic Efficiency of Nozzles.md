Nozzles increase the velocity of a fluid at the expense of pressure. Thus, since velocity (or kinetic energy) is the target, for isentropic efficiency we have:
$$
\begin{align}
\eta_{N} & =\frac{\text{Actual KE at nozzle exit}}{\text{Isentropic KE at nozzle exit}}=\frac{V_{2s}^{2}}{V_{2a}^{2}}\\[2ex] 
 & \approx \frac{h_{1}-h_{2a}}{h_{1}-h_{2s}}
\end{align}
$$
