Subcooled liquids are compressed liquids in a single phase, such that $T$ and $P$ are independent intensive properties. The effect of pressure is so small that it can be neglected in most cases (i.e. liquids are incompressible)

For a given property, $y$:
$$
y(T,P)\approx y(T) = y_{f}(T)
$$
Therefore, compressed liquids are commonly treated using saturated liquid properties at the given temperature.

Enthalpy is an exception, where:
$$
h(T,P)\approx h_{f}(T)+v_{f}(T)[P-P_{\text{sat}}(T)]
$$
