Reference states:
- Absolute zero: molecules have no motion
- Absolute vacuum: no pressure is exerted

Absolute scale for temperature:
$$
T_{\text{abs}}(K)=T(C)+273.15
$$
Pressure is typically done with Pascales:
$$
P_{\text{abs}}(\text{Pa})=P_{\text{gage}}+P_{\text{atm}}
$$
where $P_{\text{atm}}=101.325 \text{ kPa}$.
