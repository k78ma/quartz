Similar to the [[First Law of Thermodynamics for Open Systems|energy balance for open systems]], where had:
$$
\frac{dE_{\text{CV}}}{dt}=\dot{Q}-\dot{W}+\dot{m}_{\text{in}}\left( u+\frac{1}{2}V^{2}+gz \right)_{\text{in}} - \dot{m}_{\text{out}}\left( u+\frac{1}{2}V^{2} + gz \right)_{\text{out}}
$$
we can also write an entropy balance for open systems.

Recall that $\Delta S_{\text{sys}}=\sum \frac{Q_{k}}{T_{k}}+S_{\text{gen}}$ and $S_{\text{gen}}\geq 0$. The entropy balance for an open system can then be written as:
$$
\frac{dS_{\text{CV}}}{dt}=\sum \frac{\dot{Q}_{k}}{T_{k}}+\dot{m}_{\text{in}}s_{\text{in}}-\dot{m}_{\text{out}}s_{\text{out}}+\dot{S}_{\text{gen}}
$$
where $\dot{S}_{\text{gen}}\geq 0$.
