The energy in a system is balanced such that:
$$
\begin{align}
\text{Change in total energy of system}  & = \text{Total energy entering system} - \text{Total energy leaving system} \\
\Delta E_{\text{system}}  & = E_{\text{in}}-E_{\text{out}}
\end{align}
$$
Specifically, the *change of state* of [[Internal Energy|internal energy]] of a system.
