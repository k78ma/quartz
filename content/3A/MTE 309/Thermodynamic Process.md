A *process* is the change a system undergoes from one [[Equilibrium State|equilibrium state]] to another.

For example, transferring a heated ball of iron to water would be a process involving heat transfer to water.

Another example is a truck with combusted diesel exhaust cooling down to normal temperature and pressure. 
- Initial state:  ($1500\degree \text{C}, 1 \text{ atm}$)  – 2 independent, intensive properties as per the [[State Postulate]]
- Final state: ($20\degree \text{C}, 1 \text{ atm}$) – state is specified (could calculate $v$ or other properties)

This is an example of an isobaric process.

## Types of Processes
- Isobaric – constant pressure, $P$
- Isothermal – constant temperature, $T$
- Isochoric – constant specific volume, $v$
- Adiabatic – no heat transfer
