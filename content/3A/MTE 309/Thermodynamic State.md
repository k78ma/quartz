The *state* of a substance is the condition of a system at a specific time and all properties at that time  that can be measured or calculated.
## Procedure to Define State
1. Identify the phase
	- Subcooled liquid
	- Saturated liquid
	- Two-phase mixture
	- Saturated vapor
	- Superheated vapor
1. Find two independent [[Thermodynamic Properties|properties]]
	- Pressure, temperature, internal energy, enthalpy, etc…
1. Then, there will be a process (such as isobaric heating) leading to another state
