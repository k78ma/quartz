Energy can enter and leave a system by heat transfer, $Q$, which is caused by a temperature difference between the system and surroundings.
- If $T_{A} > T_{B}$, heat is transferred out of system $Q(-)$
- If $T_{B} > T_{A}$, heat is transferred into system $Q(+)$

$Q$ is in units of $[\text{kJ}]$, and $q=\frac{Q}{m} \left[\frac{\text{kJ}}{\text{kg}}\right]$. This is a *process*, not a property; it's only quantified across a boundary.
