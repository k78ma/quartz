Enthalpy is a combination [[Thermodynamic Properties|property]], also known as the "total heat".
$$
\begin{align}
h & =u+Pv \quad \quad \text{(Intensive property)} \\[2ex] 
H  & = U+PV \quad \quad \text{(Extensive property)}
\end{align}
$$
The heat required to heat up a close system isobarically (constant pressure) is the change in enthalpy:
$$
Q_{\text{in}}=\Delta H
$$
