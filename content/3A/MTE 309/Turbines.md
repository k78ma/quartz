Turbines extract work by passing fluid over a set of spinning blades.

![[Turbines.png|428]]

![[Turbines-1.png|420]]

Typically:
- Well insulated/adiabatic ($\dot{Q}\approx 0$)
- $\Delta \text{PE}\approx 0$
- $\Delta \text{KE}\approx 0$
	- KE and PE may not be 0 but are negligible compared to change in enthalpy
