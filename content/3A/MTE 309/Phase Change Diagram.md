The phase of a substance is a function of temperature, specific volume, and pressure. This is often expressed graphically with:
- Temperature-specific volume ($T$-$v$) diagrams
- Pressure-specific volume ($P$-$v$) diagrams
- Pressure-temperature ($P$-$T$) diagrams
- Pressure-specific volume-temperature ($P$-$v$-$T$) surface
## T-v Diagram
- The dotted lines on the T-v diagram represents lines of constant pressure
