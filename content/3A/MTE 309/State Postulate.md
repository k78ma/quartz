The state of a simple compressible system is completely specified by two independent [[Thermodynamic Properties|intensive properties]].

Simple compressible system – no (or negligible) electromagnetic fields, gravity, surface tension.
- Temperature $T$ and specific volume $v$ are always independent, and can always fix the state of a simple compressible system. 
- Note that $T$ and $P$ are always independent in a single-phase region, but they are not independent in a two-phase region (mixture of liquid and gas for example).
