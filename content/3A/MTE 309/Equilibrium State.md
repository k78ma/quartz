The *equilibrium state* is the state of a system containing no unbalanced driving forces *within the system*. Forces across the system boundary do not matter.

- Mechanical equilibrium – forces are in balance ($P$ uniform)
- Thermal equilibrium – no heat transfer ($T$ uniform)
- Thermodynamic equilibrium – everything in balance
