[[Compressors and Pumps|Compressors]] use work input to increase the pressure of a gas. In this case, the efficiency is given by:
$$
\begin{align}
\eta_{C} & =\frac{\text{Isentropic compressor work}}{\text{Actual compressor work}}=\frac{w_{s}}{w_{a}} \\[2ex]
	 & \approx\frac{h_{2s}-h_{1}}{h_{2a}-h_{1}}
\end{align}
$$
Note that in this case, since work is the *input*, the above equation is the inverse of the [[Isentropic Efficiency of Turbines|isentropic efficiency of a turbine]], since turbines produce work as an *output*.
