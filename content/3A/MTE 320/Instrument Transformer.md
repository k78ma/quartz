In the electric power industry, special designs of transformers are used to measure high AC voltages and currents. 

The transformers used for these purposes are called [[Potential Transformer]] (PT) and [[Current Transformer]] (CT), respectively. Note that transformers cannot be used to measure high DC voltages and currents, due to time-invariance of these quantities.
