For AC circuits, sinusoidal values such as voltage are generally represented by [[Phasors]], which are based on complex numbers, allowing us to avoid trigonometric functions.

A complex number $A$ can be represented as:
- Rectangular: 
$$
A=a+jb
$$
- Polar:
$$
A=| A|\angle \alpha
$$

![[Complex Number Representation for AC Circuits.png|500]]

The magnitude can be found with:
$$
| A |=\sqrt{ a^{2}+b^{2} }
$$
Angle:
$$
\alpha=\tan ^{-1}\left( \frac{b}{a} \right)
$$
Also note that:
$$
A=| A |e^{j\alpha}=| A |(\cos \alpha+j\sin \alpha)
$$
