There are two mechanisms for generating EMF in the presence of a magnetic field. 
- [[Faraday’s Law]] necessitates a time-varying magnetic field, but no mechanical motion
- Mechanical motion is required for [[EMF Generation with Relative Motion between Conductor and Magnetic Field]]
