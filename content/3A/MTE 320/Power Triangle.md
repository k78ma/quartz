The power triangle is a right-angle triangle that provides a graphical representation of the relationships among active, reactive, and apparent power.

The angle between the apparent and real power is the same as:
- The angle between the voltage and current phasors
- The angle between the reactance and resistance
- The angle between the complex power and the real power in the complex plane
