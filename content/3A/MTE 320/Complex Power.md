Complex power is a complex number with the real part equal to real power and the imaginary part equal to the reactive power.
$$
\begin{align}
S & =\vec{V}\vec{I}^{*}  \\
 & = ZI^{2} \\
	 & =P+jQ
\end{align}
$$
where $\vec{I}^{*}$ is the complex conjugate of $\vec{I}$.

Then, we have:
$$
\begin{align}
P=\text{Re}(S)\\
Q=\text{Im}(S)
\end{align}
$$

We can also write:
$$
\begin{align}
S  & = VI^{*} \\
	 & =IZI^{*} \\
	 & = ZI^{2} \\[2ex]
	 & =V\left( \frac{V}{Z} \right)^{*} \\[2ex]
	 & = \boxed{\frac{V^{2}}{Z^{*}}}
\end{align}
$$
