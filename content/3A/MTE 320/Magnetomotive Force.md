Magnetomotive force is described by the product of the number of turns and the current in the wire wrapped around the core’s arm. The symbol of magnetomotive force is $\mathcal{F}$ and it is given by:
$$
\mathcal{F}=NI
$$
The unit is *Ampere-turn* (A.t).
