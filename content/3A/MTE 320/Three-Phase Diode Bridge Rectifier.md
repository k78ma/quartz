Three-phase diode bridge rectifiers can handle higher power levels when compared with [[Single-Phase Diode Bridge Rectifier|single-phase diode bridge rectifiers]]. They have higher quality in the waveforms of DC side voltage and AC side currents. Three-phase diode bridge rectifiers are appropriate for industrial applications, where higher power levels are to be handled by the rectifier. 

Fig. 7-15 shows the circuit diagram of a three-phase diode bridge rectifier with resistive load.

![[Three-Phase Diode Bridge Rectifier.png]]

The average value of the output voltage, $V_{d}$, can be found as:
$$
V_{d}=1.35 V_{LL}
$$
where $V_{LL}$ is the RMS value of the line-to-line voltage of the AC source.
