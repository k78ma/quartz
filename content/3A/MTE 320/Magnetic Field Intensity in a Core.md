Based on Ampere’s law, the formula for the magnetic field intensity in the magnetic circuit is:
$$
\begin{align}
\oint \vec{H}\cdot d\vec{l} & =I_{\text{net}} \\[2ex] 
Hl & = NI \\[2ex] 
H & = \frac{NI}{l}=\frac{\mathcal{F}}{l} \quad \quad \left( \frac{A.t}{m} \right)
\end{align}
$$
where $l$ is the mean path length of the magnetic core.

According to the relation $H = \mathcal{F} / l$, magnetic field intensity represents the magnetomotive force drop across each unit of length of the core.
