For [[Ferromagnetic Materials|ferromagnetic materials]], plotting magnetic flux $\phi$ against magnetomotive force $\mathcal{F}$, or magnetic flux density $B$ against magnetic field intensity $H$ is called the [[Magnetization Curve of Ferromagnetic Materials|magnetization curve]].

In DC machines, a similar characteristic exists, but is commonly expressed as a plot of the induced emf, $E_{\text{gen}}=E_{c}=(ZP / 2\pi a)\phi \omega_{m}=(ZP / 60a)\phi n$, against field current $I_{f}$.

![[Magnetization Curve of DC Machines.png|444]]

Unless otherwise stated, a linear relationship between the field current and the induced voltage in the armature (i.e., operation in the linear region of magnetization curve) is assumed.
