Power electronics is the art of converting the electric power available from a source to that required by a load (i.e., matching the properties of the source with the requirements of the load). 

The conversion is performed by a power converter composed of semiconductor devices (diodes, switches), energy storage elements (inductors, capacitors), and isolation transformers.
