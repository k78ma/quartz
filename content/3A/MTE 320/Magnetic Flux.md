Magnetic flux, $\phi$, is defined as the number of lines of magnetic force passing through a specific area in space, $A$. The unit for magnetic flux is Weber ($\text{Wb}$).

### Magnetic Flux Density
Magnetic flux density is defined as the number of lines of magnetic force passing through unit area.
$$
B=\frac{\phi}{A}
$$
The symbol used for magnetic flux density is $B$ and its unit is $\text{Wb} / \text{m}^{2}$ or Tesla ($\text{T}$).
