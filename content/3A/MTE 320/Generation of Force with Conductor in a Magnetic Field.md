When a conductor of length $l$, carrying a current $i$, is placed in a magnetic field $B$, a force will be exerted on it which is given by:
$$
\vec{F}=\vec{i}l \times  \vec{B}
$$
In order to maximize the magnitude of the developed force, for a given current, conductor length, and magnetic flux density, the structure of electric machines are designed such that:
$$
\vec{i}\perp \vec{B}\quad \longrightarrow \quad | \vec{i}l\times \vec{B} |=i\times l\times B\times \sin(90\degree)=ilB
$$

### Right-hand Rule
The direction of the force $\vec{F}$ can be found by using right-hand fingers. If you point your right-hand fingers in the direction of $\vec{i}$, and wrap your fingers towards the direction of $\vec{B}$, your thumb will be pointing to $\vec{F}$.
