![[Electrical-Mechanical Systems Analogy.png]]

| Mechanical                        | Electrical                         |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Force (torque) $f$                | Voltage $v_s$                      |
| Velocity $v=\dot{x}$              | Current $i$                        |
| Mass (inertia) $m$                | Inductance $L$                     |
| Damping $b$                       | Resistance $R$                     |
| Stiffness $k$                     | Reciprocal of capacitance $1/C$    |
| $\omega_{n}=\sqrt{ \frac{k}{m} }$ | $\omega_{n}=\sqrt{ \frac{1}{CL} }$ |
