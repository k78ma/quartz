The second moment of area is a geometric property that provides a measure of how points are distributed with respect to an axis.

Second moment of area about $x$ axis:
$$
I_{x}=\int y^{2} \, dA
$$
Second moment of area about $y$ axis:
$$
I_{y}=\int x^{2} \, dA
$$
