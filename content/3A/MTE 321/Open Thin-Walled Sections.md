When the median wall line is closed, the section is said to be an *open section*. Open sections in torsion, where the wall is thin, have shear stress as:
$$
\tau=G\theta c=\frac{3T}{Lc^{2}}
$$
where:
- $T$ is torque
- $\tau$ is shear stress
- $c$ is wall thickness
- $G$ is modulus of rigidity
- $L$ is length of median line
- $\theta$ is angular twist per unit length
