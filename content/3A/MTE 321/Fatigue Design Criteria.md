### Infinite-life Design
- Design for stresses that never exceed the endurance limit
- Plastic strain is almost entirely avoided
- Applications that need to sustain over a million stress cycles
- Used with [[Stress-Life Method|stress-life method]]

### Safe-life Design
- Finite-life (< 1 million cycle)
- Applications may have limited stress cycles or imprecision in estimating fatigue life
- Uses significant safety margins
- Used with [[Stress-Life Method|stress-life method]] and [[Strain-Life Method|strain-life method]]

### Fail-safe design
- Design so that if one part fails, the entire system does not fail
- Not trying to entirely prevent crack initiation and growth

### Damage-tolerant design
- Assumes a crack is present
- Used with [[Linear-Elastic Fracture Mechanics Method]]
- Aimed at dictating inspection and replacement schedule
