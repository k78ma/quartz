In closed thin-walled tubes, where $t\ll r$, shear stress is given by:
$$
\tau=\frac{T}{2A_{m}t}
$$
The angular twist (radians) per unit of length of the tube is given by:
$$
\theta=\frac{TL_{m}}{4GA^{2}_{m}t}
$$
where:
- $T$ is torque
- $t$ is thickness
- $\tau$ is shear stress
- $G$ is modulus of rigidity
- $L_{m}$ is the length of section median line
- $A_{m}$ is the area enclosed by the section median line
- $\theta$ is the angle of twist per unit length of the tube
