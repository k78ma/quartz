For a member with a rectangular cross-sectional area $b \times c$, the shear stress is given as:
$$
\tau_{\text{max}}=\frac{T}{\alpha bc^{2}}\approx \frac{T}{bc^{2}}\left( 3+\frac{1.8}{b / c} \right)
$$
where:
- $b$ is the width (longer side)
- $c$ is the thickness (shorter side)
- $\alpha$ is a factor that is a function of the ratio $b / c$

The angle of twist is given by:
$$
\theta=\frac{Tl}{\beta bc^{3}G}
$$
where $\beta$ is also a factor that is a function of the ratio $b / c$.
