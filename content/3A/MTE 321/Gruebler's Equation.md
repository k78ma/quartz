Gruebler's Equation states:
$$
M=3L-2J-3G
$$
where:
- $M$ is the [[Kinematics Fundamentals|degree of freedom]] or mobility
- $L$ is the number of links
- $J$ is the number of joints
- $G$ is the number of grounded links
