The Cartesian stress components can be found by considering a single, infinitesimally small cube within the analyzed member. The goal is typically to identify the stress element with the highest stress levels.

There are nine components:
- Normal stress
	- $\sigma_{x}, \sigma_{y}, \sigma_{z}$
- Tangential shear stress
	- $\tau_{xy}, \tau_{xz}, \tau_{yx}, \tau_{yz}, \tau_{zx}, \tau_{zy}$

The normals to each surface define the $x,y,z$ axes.
