The polar moment of inertia describes a cross-section's resistance to [[Torsion|torsion]] due to its shape. 

For a hollow section it is typically given by:
$$
J=\frac{\pi}{32}(d_{o}^{4}-d_{i}^{4})
$$
where $d_{o}$ and $d_{i}$ are the outer and inner diameters.
