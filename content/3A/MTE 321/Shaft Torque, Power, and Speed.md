The power of a rotating shaft can be found by:
$$
H=T\omega
$$
where:
- $H$ is power, in watts $\text{W}$
- $T$ is torque, in $\text{N}\cdot \text{m}$
- $\omega$ is angular velocity, in $\text{rad}/\text{s}$

When dealing with RPM, we can use the conversion:
$$
T=9.55 \frac{H}{n}
$$
where $H$ is power in watts and $n$ is the angular velocity in terms of RPM.

In U.S. customary units, we have:
$$
H=\frac{FV}{33000}=\frac{2\pi Tn}{33000(12)}=\frac{Tn}{63025}
$$
where:
- $H$ is power in horsepower $\text{hp}$
- $T$ is torque in $\text{lbf}\cdot \text{in}$
- $n$ is shaft speed in $\text{rev}/\text{min}$
- $F$ is force in $\text{lbf}$
- $V$ is velocity in $\text{ft} / \text{min}$
