For dynamic loading conditions, the impact of discontinuities is not as pronounced on fatigue (dynamic) as on yield strength (static). This means that we need a different stress concentration factor, $K_{f}$, called the *fatigue stress-concentration factor*.

- For normal stress:
$$
K_{f}=\frac{\sigma_{\text{max}}}{\sigma_{0}}
$$
- For shear stress:
$$
K_{fs}=\frac{\tau_{\text{max}}}{\tau_{0}}
$$

The relationship between $K_{t}$ for static loading and $K_{f}$ for dynamic loading is defined by [[Notch Sensitivity|notch sensitivity]], $q$.
