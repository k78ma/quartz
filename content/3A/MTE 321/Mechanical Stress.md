Mechanical stress is the internal resistance offered by a unit area of a material to an externally applied load.

Types of stress:
- Normal stress – $\sigma$
	- Tension (positive)
	- Compression (negative)
- Tangential shear stress – $\tau$

SI units are Newtons per square meter – $1 \text{ N} / \text{m}^{2} = 1 \text{ Pa}$
US units are pounds per square inch – $\text{psi}$
