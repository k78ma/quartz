Given a [[Velocity Field|velocity field]] description of a fluid $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x,y,z,t)$ we can say that steady and unsteady flows can be characterized:
- Steady flow: $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x,y,z)$
- Unsteady flow: $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x,y,z,t)$

Essentially, the steady flows don't vary with time, so we don't need to parametrize with time. They can just be defined based on $\mathbf{V}$ at a certain position $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0})$. 
- $\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0},t_{1})=\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0},t_{2})$ would be a steady flow
- $\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0},t_{1})\neq\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0},t_{2})$ would be a steady flow
