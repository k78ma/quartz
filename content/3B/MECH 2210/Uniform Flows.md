A uniform flow is characterized by constant velocity in a single direction, with velocity components $u= U, v=0, w= 0$.

![[Plane Potential Flows-20250518133238567.png]]

The stream function for uniform flow is:
$$
u = U = \frac{ \partial \psi }{ \partial y } 
$$
Integrating with respect to $y$:
$$
\psi = Uy + C
$$
This means that the streamlines are parallel and spaced linearly with $y$. We can also write $\psi = Ur\sin \theta+C$ in cylindrical coordinates. 


The velocity potential for uniform flow is:
$$
u = U = \frac{ \partial \phi }{ \partial x } 
$$
Integrating with respect to $x$:
$$
\phi = Ux + C
$$
This means the equipotential lines are vertical and spaced linearly with $x$. We can also write $\phi = Ur\cos \theta+C$ in cylindrical coordinates.
