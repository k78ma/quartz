The velocity field $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x,y,z,t)$ of a fluid is given as
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbf{V} & =u\mathbf{i}+v\mathbf{j}+w\mathbf{k} \\
     & =u(x,y,z,t)\mathbf{i}+v(x,y,z,t)\mathbf{j}+w(x,y,z,t)\mathbf{k}
\end{align}
$$
The magnitude can be found with
$$
V=| \mathbf{V} |=\sqrt{ u^{2}+v^{2}+w^{2} }
$$
