The bulk modulus is used to characterize the compressibility of a fluid, and is defined as:
$$
E_{v}= - \frac{dp} {d \forall  / \forall   }
$$
where $dp$ is the differential change in pressure needed to create a differential change in volume, $d\forall$, in volume $\forall$.

![[Bulk Modulus.png|132]]

Since a decrease in volume of a given mass, $m=\rho \forall$, and mass is always constant such that $dm=\rho d\forall+\forall d\rho=0$, we have
$$
\frac{d\forall }{\forall }=-\frac{d\rho}{\rho}
$$
such that we can also write bulk modulus as
$$
E_{v}=\frac{dp}{d\rho / \rho}
$$
