In the diagram, we see a stagnation point at the front of the object. This is where the fluid velocity relative to the object is zero; at this point, the dynamic pressure is converted entirely into static pressure:
$$
p=\frac{1}{2}\rho V^{2}
$$
This represents the highest pressure on the object's surface.
