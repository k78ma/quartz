Fluids that satisfy
$$
\tau=\mu  \frac{du}{dy} \quad \text{or} \quad \tau=\left| \mu  \frac{du}{dy} \right|
$$
where $\mu$ is the [[Viscosity|viscosity]] are Newtonian fluids.

Fluids for which the shearing stress is not linearly related to the rate of shearing strain are designated as non-Newtonian fluids.
