There are two general approaches in analyzing fluid mechanics problems:
- **Eulerian method:** Fluid motion is described by fluid properties at different locations and times, using $\mathbf{V}=\mathbf{V}(x_{0},y_{0},z_{0},t)$ at fixed locations.
- **Lagrangian method:** Fluid motion is described by properties of fluid particles at different times, following individual fluid particles as they move about, using $\mathbf{V}_{A}=\mathbf{V}_{A}(t)$.

The two methods are equivalent in terms of fluid motion description. In practical applications, the Eulerian method is more popular. In some cases, Lagrangian method can be very simple.
