When gases are compressed or expanded, the relationship between pressure and density depends on the nature of the process.

- Isothermal process:
$$
\frac{p}{\rho}=\text{constant}
$$
- Isentropic process:
$$
\frac{p}{\rho^{k}}=\text{constant}
$$
where $k=c_{p}/c_{v}$, which are the [[Specific Heat|specific heats]] at constant pressure and constant volume. The two specific heats are related by the gas constant, $R=c_{p}-c_{v}$.
