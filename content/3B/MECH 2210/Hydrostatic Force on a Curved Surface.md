Consider a curved portion of the swimming pool shown in Fig. 2.23a. We wish to find the resultant fluid force acting on section BC (which has a unit length perpendicular to the plane of the paper) shown in Fig. 2.23b.

![[Hydrostatic Force on a Curved Surface.png]]

We first isolate a volume of fluid that is bounded by the surface of interest, in this instance section $BC$, the horizontal plane surface $AB$, and the vertical plane surface $AC$. Then, we have:
$$
\begin{align}
F_{1} & =\gamma h_{1}A \\
F_{2} & = \text{ pressure prism} \\
F_{H} & = F_{2} \\
F_{V} & =F_{1}+W
\end{align}
$$
and the magnitude of the resultant is
$$
F_{R}=\sqrt{ F_{H}^{2}+F_{V}^{2} }
$$
