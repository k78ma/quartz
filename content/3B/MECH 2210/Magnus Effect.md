The Magnus effect is the generation of lift due to the rotation of a cylindrical or spherical object in a fluid.

A rotating object creates a difference in relative velocities on either side:
- The side rotating with the flow increases velocity → lower pressure.
- The side rotating against the flow decreases velocity → higher pressure.
- This creates a pressure imbalance, generating lift.
