**Forward kinematics:** Given the control parameters (e.g., wheel velocities) and the time of movement $t$, find the poses $(x,y,\theta)$, reached by the robots.

![[Robot Kinematics.png|411]]

**Inverse kinematics:** Given the desired poses $(x,y,\theta)$, find the control parameters to move the robot there at a given time $t$.
