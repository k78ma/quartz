**Exteroceptive:** Measure something external to the robot.
- Active (affect environment by emitting energy)
    - Sonar range finder
    - Laser scanner
    - Depth camera
- Passive (do not affect environment)
    - Camera
    - Microphones
    - Tactile sensors

**Proprioceptive:** Measure something internal to the robot.
- Wheel odometry
- Accelerometer
- Gyroscope
- Force/torque sensor
