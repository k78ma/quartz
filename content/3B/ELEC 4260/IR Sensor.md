Infrared light has long wavelengths (700-1000nm)
- Invisible to human eyes

Reflectance sensing hardware
    - The emitter is usually made with an LED, and the detector is usually a photodiode/phototransistor 

Working principle:
    - Measure distance by the intensity of reflected light
    - It can measure ambient light (without emission) and subtract it from the reflected light

Application and weaknesses:
- Common usage: cheap robots
- Disadvantage: depends on object characteristics, e.g. shape/color
