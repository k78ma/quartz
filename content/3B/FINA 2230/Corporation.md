## Features of Corporations
- Separate legal entity (the right to sue and be sued  
in its own name)  
- Unlimited life  
- Investor ownership  
- Limited liability of the shareholders  
- Delegated management  
- Transferable shares

## Tax Implications of Corporations
- Double taxation
    - First, a corporation’s profits is subject to corporate income tax (business profits tax)
    - Then, the dividend received by the shareholder is subject to personal income tax (not applicable in HK)
- Note: Dividend income or capital gains is not subject to personal income tax in HK.
