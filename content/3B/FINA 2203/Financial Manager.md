The goal of the financial manager is to maximize the wealth of the owners and the stockholders.

Three main tasks:
1. Make investment decisions
    - Add value for its owners
2. Make financing decisions
    - Sell stocks or bonds
3. Manage cash flow from operating activities
    - Maintain enough cash from hand
