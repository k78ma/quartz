We refer to a series of cash flows lasting several periods as a stream of cash flows.

![[Value of a Cash Flow Stream.png|500]]

The present value of the cash flow stream is the sum of the present values of each cash flow:
$$
PV=C_{0}+\frac{C_{1}}{1+r}+\frac{C_{2}}{(1+r)^{2}}+\dots \frac{C_{N}}{(1+r)^{N}}
$$
