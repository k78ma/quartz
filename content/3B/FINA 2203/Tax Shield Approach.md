This approach allows us to calculate free cash flow directly by focusing on the tax savings to the firm resulted from depreciation expenses.
- Particularly useful when major FCF are the cost of equipment, e.g., in office automation, and replacement decision.
- $\text{Depreciation tax shield} = \text{Depreciation}\times t_{c}$
    - The cash flow effect of depreciation is reducing taxes.

Incremental earnings approach:
$$
\begin{align}
\text{Incremental FCF} & =(\text{Revenues}-\text{Costs}-\text{Depreciation})(1-t_{c}) \\
     & \quad  +\text{ Depreciation} \\
     & \quad - \text{ Net Capital Expenditures} \\
     & \quad - \,\Delta \text{NWC}
\end{align}
$$
Tax shield approach:
$$
\begin{align}
\text{Incremental FCF} & =(\text{Revenues}-\text{Costs})(1-t_{c}) \\
     & \quad  +\, \,t_{c}\times \text{Depreciation} \\
     & \quad - \text{ Net Capital Expenditures} \\
     & \quad  - \,\Delta \text{NWC}
\end{align}
$$
