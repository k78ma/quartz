Decision Criteria for Standalone Projects:

For projects with conventional cash flows: [[Net Present Value|NPV]] rule and [[Internal Rate of Return|IRR]] rule are consistent.
For projects with non-conventional cash flows:
- Delayed investment (positive cash flows precede negative cash flows)
    - Use NPV rule
    - IRR rule is unreliable.

Alternative Decision rule: [[Payback Rule]]
