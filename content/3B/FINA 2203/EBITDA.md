- Financial analysts often compute a firm’s earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, or EBITDA
- Because depreciation and amortization are not cash flows, this subtotal reflects the cash a firm has earned from operations net of leverage effect
- Calculate how much cash is generated from utilizing the assets
