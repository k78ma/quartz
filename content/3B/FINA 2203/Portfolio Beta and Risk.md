Portfolio risk and return can be estimated by computing portfolio beta. Systematic risk of a portfolio is based on the systematic risk of individual securities in the portfolio. The systematic risk cannot be further reduced no matter how many securities are in the portfolio.

The portfolio beta is calculated as the weighted average betas of individual assets in the portfolio:
$$
\beta_{P} = w_{1}\beta_{1}+w_{2}\beta_{2}+\dots+w_{n}\beta_{n}
$$
