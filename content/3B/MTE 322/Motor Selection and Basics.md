## Motor Selection
The first stage of motor selection typically involves applying basic principles:
- Kinematics (velocity, acceleration)
- Dynamics (torque, power)
- Choosing gears

The second stage then involves determining the type and specifications of motors:
- Different control objectives (position, velocity, force)
- Motor drives, cost, etc.
- DC (brush), AC (induction), Synchronous & stepper
