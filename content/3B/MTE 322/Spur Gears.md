Spur gears have teeth parallel to the axis of rotation and are used to transmit motion from one shaft to another parallel shaft. 

Some key points:
- They are the least expensive but are noisier than others. 
- They are high efficiency (99%/set)
- Can be disengaged
- Backlash can be a problem
