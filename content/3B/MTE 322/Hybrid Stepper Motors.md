In hybrid stepper motors, the [[Stepper Motors#Variable Reluctance Stepper Motor|VR rotor]] is constructed with a permanent magnet:

![[Hybrid Stepper Motors.png|608]]

Front stack is held by “N” poles of the stator. The back stack is held by “S” poles of the stator. Multi-stacks create more force.

![[Hybrid Stepper Motors-1.png|640]]

![[Hybrid Stepper Motors-2.png|636]]

Step angle for the case above:
$$
\frac{360\degree}{(\text{rotor teeth})\times \text{stator phases}}=\frac{360\degree}{50\times 4}=1.8\degree
$$
