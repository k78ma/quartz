Permanent magnet synchronous motors are a type of AC motor
- An overlapping coil (usually $3 \phi$) creates smooth rotating field (like an induction motor)
- The PM rotor rotates in sync with the rotating field
- For maximum efficiency, rotor is being is being monitored to adjust rotating field
- Cannot self-start by default (e.g. Adding squirrel cage bars can make it self-start)
