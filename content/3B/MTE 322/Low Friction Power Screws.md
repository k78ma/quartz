Ball screws;
- Very smooth (low friction), thus back-driveable
- High load capacity
- Popular for linear drive mechanism

![[Low Friction Power Screws.png|632]]

Roller screws:
- Replacing balls with grooved rollers
- Increase precision with extremely high load capacity, acceleration, and speed capabilities (comparable to hydraulic actuators)
