Power screws and lead screws are a drive mechanism to convert rotary motion to linear motion. They are capable of creating very large mechanical advantage from a very small electric motor; this means that the thread must be strong enough to hold up the large linear force.

![[Power Screws and Lead Screws.png|284]]

## Threads for Power Screws

- Square thread
- Acme thread
- Buttress thread
