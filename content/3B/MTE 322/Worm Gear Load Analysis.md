In a worm gear, the actual work is done through $W_{tW}$ (input) and $W_{tG}$ (output). They are related to each other as
$$
W_{tW}=W_{tG} \frac{\cos \phi_{n}\sin \lambda+\mu \cos \lambda}{\cos \phi_{n}\cos \lambda-\mu \sin \lambda}=W_{tG} \frac{\cos \phi _{n}\tan \lambda+\mu}{\cos \phi_{n}-\mu \tan \lambda}
$$
