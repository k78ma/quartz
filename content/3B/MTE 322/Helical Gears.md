## Basic Properties
- Teeth not parallel to axis  
- More expensive than spur  
- Quieter than others  
- Axial force component  
- Parallel or crossed axes  
- Stronger tooth section  
- Harder to disengage

![[Helical Gears.png|328]]

## Right vs. Left Handed
- Helical gears are mated with opposite **handedness**
- By definition, right-handed gear means that “the teeth twist clockwise as they recede from an observer looking along the axis"
