The allowable thrust load of a retaining ring is given by
$$
R_{s}=A \frac{D\pi TS_{s}}{N_{s}}
$$
- $R_{s}$ is the allowable shear load for ring $[\text{N}]$
- $A$ is the retaining ring shape factor (table)
- $S_{s}$ is the shear strength $[\text{N} / \text{mm}^{2}]$, usually 100 ~ 500 for steel
- $D$ is the shaft diameter $[\text{mm}]$
- $T$ is the ring thickness $\text{[mm]}$
- $N_{s}$ is the safety factor

The allowable thrust load of a groove
$$
G=B \frac{D\pi dG_{y}}{N_{s}q}
$$
- $G$ is the allowable thrust load in the groove $[\text{N}]$
- $B$ is the groove shape factor
- $D$ is the shaft diameter $[\text{mm}]$
- $d$ is the groove depth $[\text{mm}]$
- $G_{y}$ is the groove yield strength $[\text{N} / \text{mm}^{2}]$
- $N_{s}$ is the safety factor
- $q$ is the decreasing factor (graph below)
