Gears typically fail in two ways:
- Failure by **bending** (fatigue)
- Failure by **pitting** (surface wear)

For both cases, we have to satisfy:
$$
\sigma \leq \sigma_{\text{all}}
$$
where $\sigma$ is the actual stress and $\sigma_{\text{all}}$ is the allowed strength.
