## Encoders
Encoders are essential in robotic arms and CNC machines. Almost all wheeled mobile robots have encoders.
- Odometry data is usually given by encoder measurements
- Incremental encoders with quadrature decoding are common

![[Position Sensors-20251030100857668.png]]


## Motion Capture
Motion capture devices are often used for indoor, high-precision, absolute positioning sensors. Typically, this consists of a camera system for detecting retroreflective markers. The markers reflect light generated by the cameras. The exact locations as well as orientations of markers can be found by triangulating multiple cameras.

## GPS and GNSS
GPS and GNSS are almost essential in outdoor field robots. The European GNSS Galileo High Accuracy Service (HAS) is known to be capable of 1 cm accuracy.
