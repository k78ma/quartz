> [!theorem] Boundary Crossing Lemma
> Let $\lambda \in  [0,1]$ and $\Delta_{\lambda}[z] = \Delta_{1}[z] - \lambda\Delta_{2}[z]$. If $\Delta_{1}[z]$ is Schur and $\Delta_{1}[z]-\Delta_{2}[z]$ is not Schur (or vice versa), then $\exists \, \lambda^{\ast } \in [0,1]$  such that $\Delta \lambda^{\ast }[z]$ has a root on the unit circle.
